Announcing! 📣 ¶We are excited to announce that in ytt v0.41.0, we are including a preview of a powerful new feature: ytt Validations! What are ytt Validations? 🤔 ¶Validations are constraints that you can define on Data Values via an annotation. A Validation ensures that a Data Value is in the expected range of values.
