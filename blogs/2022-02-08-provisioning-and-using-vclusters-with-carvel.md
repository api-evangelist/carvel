Your head should hurt a little bit after reading this blog, but hopefully it’s because of all the ideas you are thinking about around Carvel and vcluster and not because you are unsure what Kubernetes cluster you are using. vcluster is a project from Loft Labs that delivers on an exciting idea: delivering the experience of using an independent Kubernetes cluster within a host Kubernetes cluster’s namespace. To summarize, your cluster can host other clusters.
